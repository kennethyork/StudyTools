Read the Bible

The whole Bible in this site's own reader — every book of the World English Bible (Updated), the modernized King James Version, and the modernized Revised Version, Apocrypha included. Switch translations as you read, and tap any verse to see it in all three translations with the cross-references, the Greek behind it, and where the Prayer Book reads it.

Start reading →

Search the Bible

Search the whole Bible — Apocrypha included — in the translations this site carries. Every word you type has to be in the verse, so shepherd want finds the verses with both. Results open in the reader or in Study a Passage, and the index is a file on your own machine: nothing you type leaves it.

Search the text →

Verse Notes

Tap a verse in the reader and write down what you see in it — a word that struck you, a cross-reference, a question. Every note gathers in one place, searchable by book or by text, and exports as Markdown or JSON so it is yours to keep. Stored in your browser; nothing sent anywhere.

Reading Plan

Read the whole Bible in a year, the New Testament in ninety days, or the Old Testament in half a year — worked out from the books this site carries, so no chapter is missed and no day is empty. Tick a day when you have read it, and today's portion is ready on the front page.

Study a Passage

Type a reference and get everything this site holds on it in one place: the text in three translations, the cross-references the church has drawn to it, the ground it shares with the Tanakh, the Qur'an and the Book of Mormon, and the original text word by word — Greek for the New Testament, Hebrew for the Old. Printable, so it works as a teaching handout. Built for preparing to teach.

Study a passage →

Parallel Passages

The Bible beside the Jewish Tanakh, the Qur'an and the Book of Mormon — 99 comparisons, each one the Bible against a single other tradition — Tanakh, Qur'an or Book of Mormon — one passage against one passage. Every book of the Tanakh and every book of the Book of Mormon is covered, each side quoted from its own text in the same present-day English.
