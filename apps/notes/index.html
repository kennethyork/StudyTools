Verse Notes

Every note you have written against a verse, in one place. Tap a verse in the reader and write; it appears here. Edit or delete one here and the reader shows the change. They are kept in this browser, and the export buttons are the way to get them out.

Notes are stored under studytools.verse-notes.v1 in this browser, like the rest of the site's private data — no account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.
