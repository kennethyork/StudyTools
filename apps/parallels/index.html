Parallel Passages

The Bible read beside the Jewish Tanakh, the Qur'an and the Book of Mormon on the same figures and events. 99 comparisons, every one of them the Bible against a single other tradition — Tanakh, Qur'an or Book of Mormon — one passage against one passage. Every book of the Tanakh and every book of the Book of Mormon is covered, and every side is quoted from its own text in the same present-day English, so you can see where the accounts agree and where they part.
