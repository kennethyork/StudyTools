Every place named in the Bible, on a map drawn from public-domain coastlines — no map tiles, nothing fetched while you read. Click a place to see the verses it appears in, each one opening in the reader.

Places, their types and the verses they appear in are from the OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding dataset (CC BY 4.0; its own sources number in the hundreds, and some identifications are contested). The coastline, lakes and rivers of the base map are Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain. The map is a plain equirectangular plot of those coordinates drawn here, not a historical atlas: it shows where the dataset puts each place today, and the borders of no period at all.
