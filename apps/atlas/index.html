Places, their types and the verses they appear in are from the OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding dataset (CC BY 4.0; its own sources number in the hundreds, and some identifications are contested). The coastline, lakes and rivers of the base map are Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain. The map is a plain equirectangular plot of those coordinates drawn here, not a historical atlas: Click anywhere on the map for the places near that point, pick a journey to follow it, or pick a book and chapter to see only the places that chapter names. It shows where the dataset puts each place today, with the borders of no period at all — the ones drawn are today's, and are there only to help you find your way. The names of the seas and the regions are this site's own, placed by hand; the coastlines, rivers, lakes, borders and modern cities are Natural Earth's.
