Read the Bible

Seven public-domain translations: the World English Bible (Updated), the modernized King James Version and the modernized Revised Version, each carrying the Apocrypha; the modernized American Standard Version and Young's Literal Translation, which keep to the sixty-six book canon; the Jewish Publication Society's Tanakh of 1917, modernized, for the Old Testament; and the modernized Douay-Rheims of 1752, which keeps the Vulgate's numbering and is read on its own. Choose a book, a chapter and a translation, then tap any verse to see it in every translation that has it, with the cross-references the church has drawn to it, the public-domain commentary the site holds on it, the Greek or Hebrew behind it, and where the Prayer Book reads it — and to write a note of your own. If your machine can run one, you can also turn on a small language model and ask it about the verse: it runs on your device, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Scripture and study data are public domain or openly licensed; the About page lists every source and its licence. Your notes, highlights and reading progress are stored in this browser and are not sent anywhere.
